Questions

Before you book, the usual questions.

Do I need to be home for the cleaning?+

No. Most clients leave entry instructions — a lockbox, code, or key — and go about their day. You'll get a text when the crew arrives and again when they're done.

What's included in a standard visit?+

Kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, and living areas — dusting, vacuuming, mopping, and disinfecting, every visit. See the full checklist above for the room-by-room breakdown.

Can I skip or reschedule a visit?+

Yes, anytime, with a text. No cancellation fee as long as we get at least 24 hours' notice.

Are cleaners background-checked?+

Every cleaner is background-checked before their first visit and covered under our liability insurance while working on your property.

Do you bring your own supplies?+

Yes — standard supplies and equipment are included in every visit. If you'd rather we use products you provide, just let us know when you book.

What if I'm not happy with a visit?+

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll send the crew back to fix it — no charge, no argument.
