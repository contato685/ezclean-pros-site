Vacation Rental Cleaning in San Diego

Turnover cleaning for vacation homes and seasonal properties: fresh linens, a full reset of the kitchen and bathrooms, and a walkthrough before the next arrival, whether that's you or a guest.

TurnoverLinen changeSeasonal

Vacation rentals live and die by their reviews, and nothing tanks a review faster than a guest walking into a property that still feels like the last group left ten minutes ago. EZClean Pros handles turnover cleaning for vacation homes and seasonal properties across San Diego: fresh linens, a full reset of the kitchen and bathrooms, and a walkthrough before the next arrival — whether that's you or a guest.

Every turnover includes a full linen change and remake, kitchen and bathroom sanitizing, trash removal, restocking of basics if you keep supplies on-site, and a final walkthrough to catch anything a first pass might miss — water spots, stray items left behind, anything out of place.

This service is built to work with your booking calendar, whether that's a handful of stays a month or a tight back-to-back schedule during peak season. Coordinating around lockbox codes and check-in/check-out times is part of the job, not an extra ask.

Running the property through Airbnb or another short-term platform with fast same-day turns? Our dedicated Airbnb turnover cleaning service is built around those tighter booking windows.

Serving vacation properties throughout San Diego, La Jolla, Pacific Beach, Chula Vista, Coronado, National City, Point Loma, Mission Valley, Clairemont, Carlsbad, and Encinitas.
