OUR CLEANING PROCESS

A Checklist That Keeps Every Cleaning Consistent

Every visit follows a detailed checklist, so the important details don’t depend on memory.

Kitchen

  • Clean and disinfect countertops
  • Clean stovetop and backsplash
  • Clean inside and outside of microwave
  • Clean refrigerator exterior and check for spills
  • Clean oven exterior
  • Clean sink and faucet
  • Wipe cabinet doors and handles
  • Clean small appliances
  • Empty trash and replace liner
  • Sweep and mop floors

Bathrooms

  • Clean and disinfect toilets
  • Scrub showers and tubs
  • Clean shower doors and fixtures
  • Clean sinks and faucets
  • Clean countertops and vanities
  • Clean mirrors
  • Wipe cabinets and handles
  • Empty trash and replace liner
  • Sweep and mop floors

Bedrooms

  • Dust furniture and surfaces
  • Vacuum rugs and carpets
  • Sweep and mop hard floors
  • Wipe light switches and door handles
  • Dust lamps and decorative items
  • Make beds / change linens when requested
  • Empty trash

Living Areas

  • Dust furniture and surfaces
  • Clean coffee tables and side tables
  • Vacuum sofas and chairs
  • Vacuum rugs and carpets
  • Sweep and mop hard floors
  • Clean windows and glass doors as needed
  • Wipe light switches and door handles
  • Dust lamps and decorative items
  • Empty trash
